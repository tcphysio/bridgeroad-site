BRIDGE ROAD PHYSIOTHERAPY — SITE MAINTENANCE NOTES
==================================================

WHERE THE BUSINESS DETAILS LIVE
-------------------------------
site-config.js is the single place to change:
  - Google Business Profile links (Maps, read reviews, leave a review)
  - the Google Place ID
  - the booking URLs
  - the Google star rating and review count shown on the homepage
  - the fee list used as a cross-check against the booking system

Phone, email and address are ALSO written into every page's HTML, because
search engines need them in the markup. If you change those, search the repo
for the old value and change it everywhere.

FEES
----
fees.html is the source of truth for pricing. No other page repeats the
numbers. If a price changes: update fees.html, update window.BRP.fees in
site-config.js, and update Halaxy.

GOOGLE REVIEWS ON THE HOMEPAGE
------------------------------
Open site-config.js and find window.BRP.google. Paste in the current rating,
the review count, today's date, and two or three genuine review excerpts.
Until you do, the homepage shows only the "Read our Google reviews" button —
nothing false is ever displayed. Never write review text yourself.

ADDING PHOTOS
-------------
Images sit in the repository root. Name them descriptively in lowercase with
hyphens, e.g. thihan-chandramohan-physiotherapist-richmond.jpeg.

For every meaningful image:
  - descriptive filename
  - alt text that describes the image and its context (not keywords)
  - width and height attributes, so the page does not jump while loading
  - loading="lazy" for anything below the fold
  - .webp where you can, landscape ~1200px wide, under ~300KB

Decorative images get alt="".

CONVERSION TRACKING
-------------------
Any element with a data-track attribute fires a named event on click. The
event names are listed in site-config.js. No third-party tracker is loaded.
If Google Analytics or GTM is added later, it picks these up automatically.

THE EMBEDDED MAP
----------------
The map on the homepage and contact page uses the official embed Google
generated for the Bridge Road Physiotherapy listing, so the pin and the info
card are always the business itself.

If the listing ever moves or the map looks wrong:

  1. Open the clinic's listing in Google Maps.
  2. Share -> Embed a map -> Copy HTML.
  3. Take the src="..." value out of that iframe (the long pb= string) and
     paste it over the iframe src in index.html and contact.html, and into
     mapEmbedUrl in site-config.js.
  4. The same embed carries the listing's coordinates: !2d is the longitude
     and !3d is the latitude. Update lat/lng in site-config.js and the "geo"
     block in the index.html schema to match.

THE ENQUIRY FORM
----------------
The contact and landing page forms post to /api/enquiry, a Vercel Function
in api/enquiry.js. It emails the enquiry through Resend and keeps no copy.
Formspree is no longer used anywhere.

Set these in Vercel -> Project -> Settings -> Environment Variables:

  RESEND_API_KEY   required, from resend.com, starts with "re_"
  ENQUIRY_TO       optional, defaults to thihan@bridgeroad.physio
  ENQUIRY_FROM     optional, defaults to
                   Bridge Road Physiotherapy <enquiries@bridgeroad.physio>

ENQUIRY_FROM has to be on a domain verified in Resend, which means adding
the DNS records Resend gives you for bridgeroad.physio. Without that, Resend
refuses the send and the patient sees an error with the phone number.

The patient's own address is set as Reply-To, so hitting reply in the inbox
goes straight back to them.

If the key is missing or Resend rejects the send, the form tells the patient
it failed and shows the phone number and email address. It never pretends an
enquiry went through when it did not.

THE GAP, AND IT IS THE IMPORTANT ONE
------------------------------------
Every photograph currently on the homepage was taken in a sports setting, and
two of them show people in cricket kit. The service card that has to speak to
the largest group of patients, "Sports & everyday injuries", is illustrated by
a photograph of a physiotherapist working with an athlete.

A visitor with a sore knee and no interest in sport should see themselves on
this page. At the moment they nearly do, and that is not the same thing.

Shots worth taking, in priority order:

  1. Thihan assessing an ordinary adult patient. Street clothes, not kit.
     Shoulder, knee or lower back. This replaces service card 01 and is the
     single highest-value photograph on the list.
  2. A patient doing a rehabilitation exercise in the gym space, again in
     ordinary clothes.
  3. Thihan in conversation with a patient, explaining something. Sitting,
     talking, not treating. This is what the clinic actually sells.
  4. A clean shot of the treatment space with a person in it.
  5. A portrait of Thihan in the Richmond clinic rather than in national kit.
     The current portrait is good but it is an Italy Cricket photograph.

Avoid: empty treatment rooms, equipment close-ups with no person, generic
massage imagery, and anything that implies every patient is an athlete.

Specification for new photographs: landscape 4:3 for service cards, at least
1200px wide, .webp, under about 120KB, descriptive filename, alt text that
describes the picture and its context.

GOOGLE REVIEWS: STILL DORMANT
-----------------------------
The homepage review section is built to show a star rating, a review count and
two or three genuine review excerpts. It shows none of them, because
window.BRP.google in site-config.js is still null. Until it is filled in, the
section renders as a heading, one line and a link to the Google profile, which
is honest rather than empty.

There is no free API that returns a live Google rating, so this is a manual
job: open the Bridge Road Physiotherapy Google Business Profile, copy the
current rating and review count, copy two or three reviews verbatim, and paste
them into site-config.js. Never write review text.
