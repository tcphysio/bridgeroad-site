BRIDGE ROAD PHYSIOTHERAPY — SITE MAINTENANCE NOTES
==================================================

WHERE THE BUSINESS DETAILS LIVE
-------------------------------
site-config.js is the single place to change:
  - Google Business Profile links (Maps, read reviews, leave a review)
  - the Google Place ID
  - the booking URLs
  - the Google star rating and review count shown on the homepage
  - the fee list used as a cross-check against the booking system

Phone, email and address are ALSO written into every page's HTML, because
search engines need them in the markup. If you change those, search the repo
for the old value and change it everywhere.

FEES
----
fees.html is the source of truth for pricing. No other page repeats the
numbers. If a price changes: update fees.html, update window.BRP.fees in
site-config.js, and update Halaxy.

GOOGLE REVIEWS ON THE HOMEPAGE
------------------------------
Open site-config.js and find window.BRP.google. Paste in the current rating,
the review count, today's date, and two or three genuine review excerpts.
Until you do, the homepage shows only the "Read our Google reviews" button —
nothing false is ever displayed. Never write review text yourself.

ADDING PHOTOS
-------------
Images sit in the repository root. Name them descriptively in lowercase with
hyphens, e.g. thihan-chandramohan-physiotherapist-richmond.jpeg.

For every meaningful image:
  - descriptive filename
  - alt text that describes the image and its context (not keywords)
  - width and height attributes, so the page does not jump while loading
  - loading="lazy" for anything below the fold
  - .webp where you can, landscape ~1200px wide, under ~300KB

Decorative images get alt="".

CONVERSION TRACKING
-------------------
Any element with a data-track attribute fires a named event on click. The
event names are listed in site-config.js. No third-party tracker is loaded.
If Google Analytics or GTM is added later, it picks these up automatically.

THE EMBEDDED MAP
----------------
The map on the homepage and contact page uses the official embed Google
generated for the Bridge Road Physiotherapy listing, so the pin and the info
card are always the business itself.

If the listing ever moves or the map looks wrong:

  1. Open the clinic's listing in Google Maps.
  2. Share -> Embed a map -> Copy HTML.
  3. Take the src="..." value out of that iframe (the long pb= string) and
     paste it over the iframe src in index.html and contact.html, and into
     mapEmbedUrl in site-config.js.
  4. The same embed carries the listing's coordinates: !2d is the longitude
     and !3d is the latitude. Update lat/lng in site-config.js and the "geo"
     block in the index.html schema to match.
