How to book online

  1. 1
    Choose your appointment. New patient or a new problem: Initial Consultation. Same problem as before: Review Consultation. Fees and appointment types
  2. 2
    Pick a day and time. Can’t see one that suits? Call or text 0458 007 583.
  3. 3
    Enter your details and confirm. Bring your health fund card to claim a rebate on the day.
