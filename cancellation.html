Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026.

Notice period

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule an appointment. This gives another patient a fair chance to use the time.

Late cancellations and missed appointments

Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice might incur a fee of up to the full appointment cost. Missed appointments might also be charged in full.

Emergencies and unexpected events

Genuine emergencies and unexpected events are considered individually. Contact the clinic as early as possible on 0458 007 583 or at thihan@bridgeroad.physio.

Rebates

Private health and Medicare rebates are not available for cancellation or missed-appointment fees.

Agreement

By booking, you agree to the clinic’s cancellation terms. These terms sit alongside the Terms and Conditions.

Appointment lengths and fees are listed on the fees and appointments page.

Need to change an appointment?

Call or email as early as you can and we will find another time.
