When you arrive

The clinic is inside Uplift Gym at 507 Bridge Road. Come in the main gym door. You do not need a membership and nobody will try to sign you up.

There is no reception desk and no waiting room magazine rack. Turn up at your appointment time, grab a seat near the entrance, and I will come out and get you.

If the door is locked or you are standing outside wondering whether you are in the right place, call or text 0458 007 583.

Parking and public transport

  • Tram. The 48 and 75 run along Bridge Road and stop a short walk from the door.
  • Train. West Richmond and East Richmond stations are both within walking distance.
  • Parking. Metered and time-limited spots on and around Bridge Road. They go early and they go fast, so the tram is usually less painful than circling.

If you have access requirements, ring ahead on 0458 007 583 and I will talk you through the entrance and the space before you come.
