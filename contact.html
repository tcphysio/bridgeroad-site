When you arrive

  1. Come in through the main Uplift Gym entrance. You do not need a gym membership.
  2. There is no reception desk. Take a seat near the entrance.
  3. Thihan meets you at your appointment time. If you cannot get in or are unsure where to go, call or text 0458 007 583.

Bring your health fund card if you want to claim a rebate on the day.

Parking and public transport

  • Tram. Routes 48 and 75 run along Bridge Road, with stops close to the clinic.
  • Train. West Richmond and East Richmond stations are both within walking distance.
  • Parking. Metered and time-limited street parking is available on Bridge Road and nearby streets. Allow extra time during busy periods.

If you have access requirements, contact me before the appointment so we can discuss the entrance and clinic space.
