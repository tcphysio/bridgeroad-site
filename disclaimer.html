Website Disclaimer

The information on this website is general information. It does not replace an individual assessment, diagnosis or treatment from a qualified health professional.

Last updated: 20 September 2026.

No treating relationship from website use

Reading this website, using a link or submitting an enquiry does not create a physiotherapist-patient relationship. A treating relationship begins after an appointment is accepted and attended.

Urgent or serious symptoms

Bridge Road Physiotherapy is not an emergency service. Call 000 or seek urgent medical care for a medical emergency or serious symptoms such as chest pain, breathing difficulty, sudden neurological symptoms, suspected fracture or severe injury.

Individual results vary

Recovery and physiotherapy outcomes differ between people. Examples and general timeframes on this website do not promise a result, recovery period or return date.

External links

This website links to third-party services, including online booking, payment, enquiry and mapping services. Bridge Road Physiotherapy is not responsible for the content, availability or privacy practices of external websites.
