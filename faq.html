FAQ

Questions before your appointment

If your question is not answered here, call 0458 007 583 or send an enquiry. It comes directly to Thihan.

No referral is needed for a private physiotherapy appointment. A valid GP referral is required if you attend under a Medicare Chronic Disease Management plan.

Yes. Most patients are not elite athletes. The clinic treats back and neck pain, shoulder and knee problems, muscle and tendon injuries, work-related symptoms and many other everyday musculoskeletal problems.

We discuss what has happened, what affects the problem and what you want to return to. I assess the relevant movement, strength and function, explain the findings and agree on the first steps with you. Treatment begins on the day where appropriate.

Initial consultations are 45 minutes. Review consultations are 30 minutes. Extended consultations are 45 minutes and Return to Performance Assessments are 60 minutes. Online initial consultations are 45 minutes and online reviews are 30 minutes.

View current fees.

Choose an initial consultation if you are new to the clinic or have a new problem. Choose a review if we are already working on the same problem. The fees page explains the extended and Return to Performance appointments. If you remain unsure, call 0458 007 583.

Wear clothes you feel comfortable moving in and that allow the relevant area to be assessed. Bring useful scan reports, referral letters, operation notes and your current exercise program. Bring your health fund card if you want to claim a rebate on the day.

Yes. Eligible extras rebates are processed through Tyro Health at the appointment, and you pay the remaining gap. Your rebate depends on your fund and level of cover.

Medicare Chronic Disease Management plans are accepted with a valid GP referral, with a gap payable. WorkCover, TAC and DVA are not accepted at Bridge Road Physiotherapy.

Yes. Online initial consultations are 45 minutes and online reviews are 30 minutes. They suit many rehabilitation reviews, exercise progressions and people unable to attend in person. Some problems need an in-person examination or medical review. I will advise you if your presentation needs one.

Metered and time-limited street parking is available on and around Bridge Road. Allow extra time during busy periods. Tram routes 48 and 75 run along Bridge Road, and West Richmond and East Richmond stations are within walking distance.

View full travel and arrival details.

Enter through the main Uplift Gym entrance at 507 Bridge Road. Take a seat near the entrance and I will meet you at your appointment time. There is no clinic reception desk and no gym membership is required. If the door is locked or you are unsure where to go, call or text 0458 007 583.

Yes. A parent, partner, carer, interpreter or coach is welcome with your consent. For junior patients, a parent or guardian should attend as required.

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice. Late cancellations and missed appointments might incur a fee of up to the full appointment cost. Genuine emergencies are considered individually.
