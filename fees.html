Rebates and claiming

  • Private health. Claimed on the spot through Tyro Health, so you pay the gap and nothing else. Bring your fund card.
  • Medicare. Chronic Disease Management plans are fine with a valid GP referral. The rebate comes off on the day and you pay the gap between that and the fee.
  • No referral needed for a normal private appointment. You only need one for specific funding arrangements, like a CDM plan.
  • WorkCover, TAC and DVA are not accepted here. Call me anyway and I will point you at someone who does them properly.

What each appointment involves

Initial consultation · 45 minutes · $180

Your first visit, or a brand new problem.

Most of this one is me listening and testing. By the end you will know what I think it is, how confident I am about that, and what we are doing next. You leave with something to start on, not a follow-up booking and a vague feeling.

Review consultation · 30 minutes · $140

Once we are underway.

We check what has changed, retest the things that matter, and push the plan along. If something is not working, this is where we find out and change it.

Extended consultation · 45 minutes · $180

When thirty minutes will not cut it.

For complicated problems, more than one thing going on, a full reassessment, or a session that needs real gym work rather than a table and a plinth.

Return to Performance Assessment · 60 minutes · $250

For athletes going from feeling good to actually ready.

Feeling good and being match-fit are different things, and the space between them is where most re-injuries happen. I test what you have got against what your sport actually asks of you, then tell you what you are ready for, what is not there yet, and roughly how long the rest will take.

Online consultations

Initial consultation, online · 45 minutes · $150

New patients who cannot get in, or would rather not.

We go through the history and what you want back, then I take you through movement and strength testing on camera. Same explanation, same plan, minus the hands-on part.

Review consultation, online · 30 minutes · $120

Keeping your rehab moving from wherever you are.

Progress check, changes to the program, and the next block of work. Useful when you are travelling, mid-season, or do not need to be in the room.

Not sure which one? Book the initial consultation. If it turns out you needed something else, we sort that out at the appointment. Or call 0458 007 583 and ask.

What to bring

  • Clothes you can move in. Shorts if it is a leg problem.
  • Scan reports, if you have them. The report is worth more to me than the pictures.
  • Your referral or care plan, if there is one.
  • Your health fund card, if you want to claim on the day.

Cancellation policy

Twenty-four hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule, please. Inside that, and a fee of up to the full consultation cost may apply. A missed appointment may be charged in full.

Life happens, and I am not precious about it. Let me know as early as you are able and we will work it out.
