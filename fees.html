Rebates and referrals

  • Private health. Eligible extras rebates are processed on the spot through Tyro Health. Bring your health fund card and pay the remaining gap.
  • Medicare. Medicare Chronic Disease Management plans are accepted with a valid GP referral. The rebate is processed on the day and a gap applies.
  • Private appointments. You do not need a referral.
  • WorkCover, TAC and DVA. These schemes are not accepted at the clinic. Contact me if you need help finding a suitable service.

What each appointment is for

Initial consultation, in person

45 minutes, $180

Choose this for your first visit or a new problem. We discuss the history, assess the relevant movement and strength, and agree on the first stage of treatment and rehabilitation. You should leave knowing what I think is happening and what to do next.

Review consultation, in person

30 minutes, $140

Choose this once we have started a plan. We review progress, repeat useful tests, provide treatment where it helps and update the next stage of rehabilitation.

Extended consultation, in person

45 minutes, $180

Choose this for a complex problem, more than one area, a full reassessment or a rehabilitation session that needs additional testing or gym time.

Return to Performance Assessment

60 minutes, $250

Choose this for late-stage rehabilitation or return-to-sport planning. Testing is selected around your injury and sport. You receive a summary of your current readiness, any remaining gaps and the next progression.

Online consultations

Initial consultation, online

45 minutes, $150

Choose this if you are new to the clinic and an online assessment suits your location or circumstances. We discuss the history and goals, work through suitable movement and strength tests on video, then set out the first plan. Some injuries still need an in-person examination or medical review, and I will tell you if so.

Review consultation, online

30 minutes, $120

Choose this to review progress, update exercises and plan the next block of rehabilitation. It works well for travel, busy periods and stages where hands-on assessment is not needed.

Not sure which appointment to choose? Book an initial consultation or call 0458 007 583.

What to bring

  • Clothes that allow the relevant area to be assessed. Shorts are useful for a leg problem.
  • Relevant scan reports, referral letters or operation notes.
  • Your current exercise or training program if it relates to the problem.
  • Your referral or care plan, if you have one.
  • Your private health card if you want to claim on the day.

Cancellation summary

Give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or move an appointment. The cancellation policy explains late cancellation fees and emergencies.
