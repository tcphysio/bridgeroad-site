Rebates and claiming

  • Private health. Eligible extras rebates are claimed on the spot through Tyro Health, so you only pay the gap. Bring your health fund card.
  • Medicare. Chronic Disease Management (CDM) plans are accepted with a valid GP referral. The rebate is claimed on the day and a gap applies between the rebate and the fee.
  • No referral needed for a private appointment. A referral is only required for specific funding arrangements.
  • WorkCover, TAC and DVA are not currently accepted. Get in touch and I will point you toward a clinic that does.

What each appointment involves

Initial consultation · 45 minutes · $180

For new patients or a new injury.

Your first appointment gives us time to understand what is going on, assess your injury and discuss what you want to get back to. You will leave with a clear explanation and a practical treatment and rehabilitation plan.

Initial consultation, telehealth · 45 minutes · $150

For new patients who prefer an online appointment, or who cannot get to the clinic.

We will discuss your symptoms, injury history and goals, then work through appropriate movement and functional assessments by video. You will receive an explanation of the likely problem and a clear plan for your next steps.

Review consultation · 30 minutes · $140

For existing patients continuing treatment or rehabilitation.

We will review your progress, reassess what matters and progress your treatment and rehabilitation based on how you are responding.

Review consultation, telehealth · 30 minutes · $120

For existing patients continuing their rehabilitation remotely.

We will review your progress, modify your program where needed and plan the next stage of your recovery.

Extended consultation · 45 minutes · $180

For existing patients who need more time.

Best suited to complex presentations, multiple problems, detailed reassessment, or appointments needing additional rehabilitation or gym-based work.

Return to Performance Assessment · 60 minutes · $250

For athletes progressing from rehabilitation back to training, competition or full performance.

We will assess the physical demands of your sport against your current capacity, using relevant strength, movement and functional testing. You will receive clear recommendations on what you are ready for, what still needs work, and how to progress towards full performance.

Not sure which one to book? Choose the initial consultation, or call 0458 007 583 and ask.

What to bring

  • Clothing you can move and be assessed in. Shorts help for lower-limb problems.
  • Any relevant scans, imaging or reports. The written report matters more than the images.
  • A referral or care plan, if you have one.
  • Your private health card, if you want to claim on the day.

Cancellation policy

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice may be charged up to the full consultation fee. Missed appointments may be charged at the full consultation fee.

Genuine emergencies happen. Get in touch as soon as you can if something unexpected comes up.
