Rebates

What you pay after a rebate.

  • Private health. Eligible extras rebates are claimed on the spot through Tyro Health, so you pay the gap only. Bring your fund card.
  • Medicare. Chronic Disease Management plans are accepted with a valid GP referral. The rebate is claimed on the day and a gap applies between the rebate and the fee.
  • No referral needed for a private appointment. A referral is only for specific funding arrangements.
  • WorkCover, TAC and DVA are not accepted. Get in touch and I will point you towards a clinic that takes them.

Good to know

Before you come in.

What to bring

  • Clothing you can move and be assessed in. Shorts help for lower-limb problems.
  • Any relevant scans, imaging or reports. The written report matters more than the images.
  • A referral or care plan, if you have one.
  • Your private health card, if you want to claim on the day.

Cancellations

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Less than 24 hours’ notice may be charged up to the full consultation fee, and so may a missed appointment.

Genuine emergencies happen. Get in touch as soon as something unexpected comes up and we will sort it out.
