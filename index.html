Thihan Chandramohan · Physiotherapist

Experienced care. One physio from start to finish.

I’m Thihan Chandramohan, a physiotherapist with more than 20 years in practice and 15 years working in elite sport.

Bridge Road Physiotherapy is my Richmond clinic. You see me from your first assessment through your rehabilitation, without being handed between clinicians.

My job is to understand what is limiting you, explain it clearly and build a plan around what you need to get back to. Hands-on treatment has a place in that. It is a start, not the whole plan.
