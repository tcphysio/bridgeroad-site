Thihan Chandramohan, physiotherapist

Experienced care, one physio from start to finish.

I am Thihan, a physiotherapist with more than 20 years of clinical experience and more than 15 years in elite sport.

Bridge Road Physiotherapy is my clinic in Richmond. You see me at every appointment, from the first assessment through to the last stage of rehabilitation.

My role is to work out what is limiting you, explain it in plain language and help you decide what to do next. Treatment might include hands-on care, exercise, changes to training or work, and a gradual return to the activity you miss. Most often, it is a sensible mix of those things.

How it works

A clear process from the first appointment

01

Understand the problem

We discuss what happened, what affects it and what you need to return to. I assess the movement, strength and physical demands involved.

02

Make sense of it

I explain what the findings mean, where there is uncertainty and what I think should happen next.

03

Build the plan

We start treatment and rehabilitation, then adjust the plan as your symptoms, capacity and goals change.

Common questions

The things people most often ask before booking. There are more on the FAQ page.

No referral is needed for a private physiotherapy appointment. A valid GP referral is required if you attend under a Medicare Chronic Disease Management plan.

Yes. Most patients are not elite athletes. The clinic treats back and neck pain, shoulder and knee problems, muscle and tendon injuries, work-related symptoms and many other everyday musculoskeletal problems.

Choose an initial consultation if you are new to the clinic or have a new problem. Choose a review if we are already working on the same problem. The fees page explains the extended and Return to Performance appointments. If you remain unsure, call 0458 007 583.

Yes. Eligible extras rebates are processed through Tyro Health at the appointment, and you pay the remaining gap. Your rebate depends on your fund and level of cover.

Medicare Chronic Disease Management plans are accepted with a valid GP referral, with a gap payable. WorkCover, TAC and DVA are not accepted at Bridge Road Physiotherapy.
