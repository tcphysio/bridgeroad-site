How it works

What happens when you book

The same structure every time, whether it is your first session or your tenth.

01

Assess

We talk through what happened and what you want to get back to, then I test movement and strength to reach a diagnosis.

02

Explain and plan

You leave knowing what I think is going on, how confident I am, and what the first steps are. Treatment on the day where it helps.

03

Progress

Follow-ups re-test, progress the plan and troubleshoot — shifting toward a full return to training or sport.

No referral is needed for a private appointment. You can book directly online or by phone. A referral is only required for specific funding arrangements, such as a Medicare CDM plan.

Yes. Bridge Road Physiotherapy uses Tyro Health, so eligible private health fund rebates are claimed instantly at the time of your appointment. You only pay the gap.

Yes. If your GP has set up a Chronic Disease Management plan, bring the referral to your first session. The Medicare rebate is claimed on the spot and a gap applies between the rebate and the session fee. Details are on the sessions and fees page.

Not currently. Bridge Road Physiotherapy does not treat under WorkCover, TAC or DVA arrangements. If your injury falls under one of these schemes, get in touch and I will happily point you toward a clinic that does.

The clinic is inside Uplift Gym at 507 Bridge Road. Come in at your appointment time and take a seat in reception — no gym membership is needed. If the door happens to be locked, give the clinic a call on 0458 007 583 and Thihan will let you in.

Wear or bring clothes you would train in — shorts are ideal for lower-limb problems. Bring any scan reports, referral letters or current exercise programs. The written reports matter more than the images themselves.
