What people come in with

Physiotherapy for sport and everyday injuries

You do not need to be an elite athlete to receive thoughtful, structured care. Bridge Road Physiotherapy works with Richmond locals who want to move with less restriction and return to work, training, sport or daily life.

  • Running injuries
  • Gym injuries
  • Knee pain
  • Shoulder pain
  • Back pain
  • Cricket injuries
  • Return to sport
  • Persistent musculoskeletal problems

If physiotherapy is not the right next step, Thihan will explain why and help you work out where to go next.

Your first visit

What happens at your first appointment?

  1. Understand the problem

    Talk through your symptoms, goals, training, work and relevant health history.

  2. Assess what matters

    Examine the movements and physical factors linked to what you want to return to.

  3. Leave with a plan

    Receive a clear explanation and a practical plan. Treatment and exercise are included where appropriate.

Why here

Experienced care without the production line

  • You see the same physiotherapist throughout your care.
  • Your plan is built around the activities you want to return to.
  • The clinic sits inside Uplift Gym on Bridge Road, giving rehabilitation room to progress beyond the treatment table.

Your physiotherapist

Meet Thihan Chandramohan

Thihan is an AHPRA registered physiotherapist with more than 20 years of clinical experience. More than 15 of those years have been spent in elite sport, mainly professional and international cricket, along with consulting work for AFL teams. He now brings the same structured approach to people living, working and training around Richmond.
