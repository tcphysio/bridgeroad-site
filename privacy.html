Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Bridge Road Physiotherapy collects, uses, stores and shares personal and health information.

SummaryInformation we collectHow we use itWhen information is sharedSystems used by the clinicSecurity and retentionAccess and correctionPrivacy concerns
Last updated: 23 September 2026. Bridge Road Physiotherapy operates inside Uplift Gym at 507 Bridge Road, Richmond VIC 3121.

Information we collect

We collect the information needed to provide physiotherapy care and operate the clinic. This might include:

  • your name, date of birth and contact details;
  • health history, current symptoms, medications and relevant activity information;
  • appointment history, clinical notes and treatment plans;
  • referral, billing and payment information;
  • messages, forms and other communication with the clinic.

How we use the information

We use personal and health information to:

  • assess and treat your condition;
  • communicate about appointments and care;
  • process payments, claims and receipts;
  • maintain clinical records required for health practice;
  • meet legal and professional obligations;
  • improve clinic administration and patient experience.

We do not sell personal information. Health information is not used for marketing without consent.

When information is shared

Relevant information might be shared with your GP, specialist, insurer, funding body or another treating health professional where needed for your care, a claim or a legal requirement. Information might also be disclosed where required by law or in response to a serious threat to health or safety.

Where practical, I will discuss clinical information sharing with you first.

Systems used by the clinic

The clinic uses third-party services for practice management, bookings, website enquiries, email, payments and website hosting. These include Halaxy for bookings and practice management, Tyro Health for private health and Medicare claiming, and Resend and Vercel for website enquiries and hosting. Each service receives only the information needed for its role.

Website analytics and advertising

This website uses Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics and Google Ads measurement tools. They use cookies to record how visitors use the site, such as the pages viewed, the type of device and whether a visitor clicks to book, call or email. This tells the clinic which advertising and pages lead to appointments.

These tools do not receive the contents of the enquiry form, your booking details or any health information. The booking calendar is run by Halaxy and is covered by its own privacy policy.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on and manage ad personalisation in My Ad Center.

Secure clinical documentation tools, including AI-assisted scribing software such as Heidi, might be used to help prepare clinical notes. Thihan reviews clinical notes before they are saved. These tools support record-keeping and are not used to market to you.

Security and retention

Reasonable steps are taken to protect personal and health information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. Health records are retained for the periods required under Victorian law and relevant professional standards.

Access and correction

You may request access to your personal or health information or ask for inaccurate information to be corrected. Email thihan@bridgeroad.physio.

Privacy concerns

Please contact Bridge Road Physiotherapy first if you have a privacy concern so it may be reviewed and addressed. You may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or the Office of the Health Complaints Commissioner in Victoria, depending on the issue.
