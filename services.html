Services

Physiotherapy for everyday life, exercise and sport

People come in because pain or injury has started getting in the way. It might affect sleep, work, the gym, a weekend run or a season of sport. The starting point is the same: understand the problem, identify what you need to return to and make a plan you understand.

Sports and everyday injuries

You do not need to play sport to come here. This service covers the problems seen in most physiotherapy clinics: back and neck pain, sore shoulders and knees, ankle injuries, muscle strains, tendon pain and symptoms linked with work or daily activity.

I assess what is sore, what has changed and what the problem is stopping you from doing. Treatment then focuses on settling symptoms where needed and rebuilding the movement, strength or tolerance your goal requires.

What you leave with
An explanation, a working plan and a clear first step.
Who it suits
Richmond locals, active adults and anyone whose pain has begun to limit normal life.

Cricket injuries

Cricket places unusual demands on the body. Bowling, throwing, batting and repeated sprinting each need a different rehabilitation plan.

Common presentations include hamstring and side strains, lower-back pain and bone stress in fast bowlers, shoulder problems and injuries linked with a sudden increase in training or match load.

I assess both the injury and the cricket workload around it. Rehabilitation then builds towards the demands of your role, with a staged return to bowling, batting or fielding where needed.

What you leave with
A cricket-specific plan, sensible workload guidance and clear return criteria.
Who it suits
Junior, social and club cricketers, as well as players managing recurring or complex injuries.

Gym and lifting injuries

Shoulder, back and knee symptoms often appear after a change in exercise, technique, volume or intensity. The answer is not always to stop training.

I assess the painful movement, your current program and the load you have been managing. Where it is safe, we modify training so you keep doing useful work while rebuilding capacity. Being inside Uplift Gym gives us room to test and progress the lifts involved.

What you leave with
A practical training modification and a route back to normal lifting.
Who it suits
Gym members, strength trainees and anyone whose symptoms appear under load.

Running injuries

Running problems often reflect a mismatch between recent training and what the body is ready to tolerate. Common examples include shin, knee, calf, Achilles and hip pain.

We review your symptoms, training history, recent changes and relevant strength or movement findings. From there, I set out what to reduce, what to keep and how to build running again.

What you leave with
A staged return-to-running plan with useful weekly targets.
Who it suits
New runners, regular recreational runners and competitive athletes across all distances.

Return to sport and performance

Pain settling is useful, but it does not always mean you are ready for full training or competition. Late-stage rehabilitation closes the gap between feeling better and handling the demands of your sport.

I use relevant strength, power, movement and sport-specific testing to guide progression. The measures depend on the injury and the sport. Time since injury matters, but it is not the only test.

What you leave with
Clear measures, remaining priorities and a staged return plan.
Who it suits
Athletes returning from a significant injury, surgery or a long period away from full training.
